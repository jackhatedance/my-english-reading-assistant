Learner's definition of WEARY
[also more weary; most weary]
1 
: lacking strength, energy, or freshness because of a need for rest or sleep : tired（因需要休息或睡眠而）疲劳的，疲倦的，精神不振的
  • I need to rest my weary eyes.我需要让疲劳的眼睛休息一下。
  • The miners were weary after a long shift.矿工们干完长班之后都很疲惫。
2 
: bored or annoyed by something because you have seen it, heard it, done it, etc., many times or for a long time厌倦的；不耐烦的
  • She was weary from years of housework.多年的家务活儿让她感到厌倦。
  • a weary sigh [=a sigh that shows that someone is weary]一声疲惫的叹息
— often + of
  • I'm weary of fighting. Let's try to get along.我讨厌争斗，我们尽量和睦相处吧。
  • a professor who had grown weary of academia and wanted to try something different对学术界日趋厌倦、一心想尝试不同事物的教授
— sometimes used in combination有时用于合成词
  • winter-weary travelers厌烦冬天的旅客
— see also world-weary
3 
literary : causing you to feel tired使人疲劳的；令人厌烦的
  • the long, weary [=tiring] journey home漫长而乏味的返乡之旅

— wearily

/ˈwirəli/ adverb
  • They trudged wearily down the trail.他们缓慢吃力地沿着小径走去。
  • “It's almost over,” she said wearily.“快要结束了。”她疲惫地说。

— weariness

/ˈwirinəs/ noun [noncount]
  • His weariness showed in his face.他一脸倦容。

Learner's definition of WEARY
[+ object] somewhat formal
: to make (someone) very tired使疲劳；使疲倦
  • The work wearies me sometimes.工作有时使我疲惫。

weary of

[phrasal verb]
weary of (something)
: to become bored by (something) : to stop being interested in (something)（对…）失去兴趣，感到厌烦（或厌倦）
  • She realized that she had wearied of [=tired of] the city.她意识到自己对这座城市已感到厌倦了。
  • He quickly wearied of answering their questions.他很快就厌烦了回答他们的问题。
