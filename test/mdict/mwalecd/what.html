1 what
/ˈwɑːt/ /ˈwʌt/
pronoun
Learner's definition of WHAT
1 
a  — used to ask for information about someone or something什么（用于询问某人或某事）
  • What is your name?你叫什么名字？
  • What are those things on the table?桌上那些是什么东西？
  • What's your family like?你的家庭是什么样的？
  • What happened?发生什么事了？
  • What [=how much] does it cost?多少钱？
  • “What did she say?” “She said yes.”“她说什么？”“她说好的。”
  • What do you think of my idea?你觉得我的想法怎么样？
  • What do you want to do tonight?今晚你想做什么？
  • “Dad!” “What (do you want)?” “You have a phone call.”“爸爸！”“什么事？”“有你的电话。”
  • What (on earth) are you doing?!你（究竟）在做什么？！
  • I finished dusting. Now what (should I do)?我掸过灰尘了。现在我该做什么？
  • What (should I do) next?下面（我该）做什么？
  • What else did he say?他还说什么了？
  • You have money, fame, and a beautiful family. What more could you want?你拥有金钱、名誉和美满的家庭。你还想要什么？
b  — used to describe a question什么（用于描述问题）
  • Please ask them what they want for dinner.请问问他们晚餐想吃什么。
  • They asked her what she knew about him.他们向她询问她对他的了解。
  • I wonder what his motives were.我想知道他的动机是什么。
2 
a  — used to ask someone to say something again because you have not clearly heard or understood it什么（因未听清或未能理解而请求对方重复或解释）
  • What did you say? = (informal) What?你说什么？
— often used to show surprise about the thing that someone has just said什么（常用于对对方所说的事表达吃惊）
  • “And then I said that he could go to hell.” “You said what?!”“然后我说他去见鬼吧。”“你说了什么？！”
  • She did what?!她做了什么？！
  • What? I don't believe it. You must be joking!什么？我不相信。你肯定是在开玩笑！
b  informal — used to express surprise, excitement, etc.什么（表示吃惊、兴奋等）
  • What, no breakfast?什么？没有早餐？
  • “Have you heard? They won!” “What!? That's great!”“你听说了吗？他们赢了！”“什么？！那真是太好了！”
3 
informal — used to ask what someone's last name is什么（用于询问某人的姓）
  • “Her name's Kathy.” “Kathy what?”“她的名字叫凯西。”“凯西什么？”
4 
a  : that which : the one or ones that…的东西
  • He has no income but what he gets from his writing. [=he has no income except for the income he gets from his writing]除了写作得来的钱，他没有其他收入。
  • “Do you have any other sizes?” “No, only what you see here.”“你们还有其他尺码吗？”“没有了，只有你眼前这些了。”
b  : the kind that : the same as和…一样的；…之类
  • The speech was very much what everyone expected.那次演讲不负众望。
  • My memory isn't what it used to be. [=it is not as good as it used to be]我的记忆力不如以前好了。
c  : something that…之类的某事物
  • The dog is chewing on what appears to be a sock.那只狗正在咬像是只袜子的东西。
  • It was the beginning of what turned out to be a long and successful career.这正是日后漫长且成功的职业生涯的开端。
d  : the thing or things that…的事物；…的事情
  • What you need is a vacation. [=you need a vacation]你需要的是一个假期。
  • What made me angry was how he treated you. [=it was the way he treated you that made me angry]让我生气的是他对待你的方式。
  • What matters most is your safety. = Your safety is what matters most.最重要的是你的安全。
  • Romance novels are what she enjoys reading.她爱读的是言情小说。
  • Do what you're told.做要你做的事情。
  • Tell me what you're looking for.告诉我你在找什么。
  • She is looking for something but I don't know what.她在找东西，但我不知道她在找什么。
  • He knows what he should do.他知道该做什么。
— often followed by to + verb常接动词不定式
  • Stop telling me what to do.别再对我指手画脚。
  • I don't know what to think/say/believe.我不知道该想/说/相信什么。
  • She has (got) what it takes [=she has the skills and personality] to do the job.她具备做这份工作所需要的各方面素质。
  • I'll do what it takes to win.我会不惜代价争取胜利。
  • You'll never guess what happened to me today. [=you'll be surprised by what happened to me today]你绝对猜不到我今天遇到的事情。
◊ Guess what is often used to tell someone that you have surprising news.*guess what常用于告诉某人一个令人吃惊的消息。
  • Guess what happened to me today!猜猜我今天遇到什么事情了！
  • “Guess what!” “What?” “I bought a new car.”“你知道吗！”“什么？”“我买了辆新车。”
e  : anything or everything that : whatever无论什么
  • Say what you will, my opinion won't change. [=nothing you can say will change my opinion]无论你说什么，我的看法都不会改变。
  • Take what you need. [=take anything that you need]你需要什么就拿吧。
5 
informal — used to direct attention to something that you are about to say用于提示要说的事情
  • I'll tell you what I'm going to do: I'm going to let you have it for 30 percent off.我告诉你我要做什么，我要给你打七折。
  • I'll tell you what. If he thinks I'm cleaning up this mess, he's wrong.我告诉你，要是他以为我会收拾这个烂摊子，那他就错了。
  • Tell you what—let's eat out tonight.跟你说件事——今晚我们出去吃吧。
  • “It's not worth the money.” “You know what—you're right.”“这不值那么多钱。”“你知道吗——你说对了。”

or what

informal
1 
— used to ask about what is happening, being done, etc.还是别的什么（用于询问正在发生或者正在做的事）
  • Is it snowing, raining, or what?是在下雪，下雨，还是其他什么情况？
  • So are you ready to leave or what?因此你准备要离去还是什么？
2 
— used to ask if someone agrees with you是吧，对吧（用于询问别人是否同意）
  • Is this exciting or what? [=isn't this exciting?; don't you agree that this is exciting?]这太让人激动了，对吧？

what about

1 
a  : does that include (someone) : how about (someone)包括（某人）吗
  • “We're all going to the beach.” “What about Kenny?”“我们要去海滩。”“肯妮也去吗？”
b  : how does that affect (someone or something) : what should be done about (someone or something)…怎么办
  • “I need to leave—something has come up.” “What about the meeting?” “We can reschedule it.”“我要走了——有点急事。”“会议怎么办？”“我们可以改期。”
  • “You can throw this one away.” “What about the others?” “Those I want to keep.”“你可以扔掉这个。”“其他那些怎么办？”“那些我想留着。”
  • (And/But) What about the people who can't afford health insurance?（那么/但是）那些交不起医疗保险的人怎么办？
2 
— used to make a suggestion about what could be done…怎么样（用于提建议）
  • What about [=how about] coming with us?和我们一起去怎么样？
  • What about driving to the coast for the weekend?周末开车去海边怎么样？
  • We'll need to talk about this again. What about (meeting) next week?我们需要再次讨论这件事。下个星期（见）怎么样？
  • What about another game? [=would you like to play another game?]再玩一局怎么样？
3 
— used to ask someone to tell you something in response to the thing that you have just said你呢（用于请某人回应刚才你所说的事）
  • I like skiing and hiking. What about you? [=what sports do you like?]我喜欢滑雪和徒步旅行，你呢？
  • Everyone else is coming. What about you? [=are you coming, too?]其他人都来，你来吗？

what…for

: for what purpose or reason有何用处；为何目的
  • What did you do that for? [=why did you do that?]你为什么做这件事呢？
  • What is this switch for? [=what does this switch do?]这个开关是用来做什么的？
  • “The principal wants to see you.” “What for?” [=why?]“校长要见你。”“为什么呢？”

  • “He's nice enough, I suppose. But he's poor.” “What if he is poor? I love him!”“他人挺好的，我认为。但是他穷。”“即使他穷又怎样？我爱他！”

informal

what's what

informal
: the true state of things : the things that need to be known or understood in order to make good judgments, decisions, etc.事情的真相；事情的原委
  • She knows what's what when it comes to fashion. [=she knows a lot about fashion]谈到时装，她如数家珍。
  • We need to find out what's what.我们需要查出事情的真相。

  • He told me to go away. What's up with that? [=why did he do that?]他叫我走开。这是为什么？

adjective
Learner's definition of WHAT
always used before a noun
1 
— used to ask someone to indicate the identity or nature of someone or something什么（用于询问某人或某事物的性质或本质）
  • What fool told you that?哪个傻瓜告诉你的？
  • What book did you read?你读过什么书？
  • What news have you heard from him?你从他那儿听到了什么消息？
  • In what way are these two stories the same?这两部小说在哪方面相同？
  • We won the war, but at what price? [=did we lose or give up too much in order to win the war?]我们是赢了战争，但是付出了多大的代价呢？
2 
— used to say that someone or something is remarkable for having good or bad qualities多么（好或坏）的
  • What a good idea!多好的主意啊！
  • What mountains!多么雄伟的山啊！
  • Remember what fun we had?记得那时我们玩得多开心吗？
  • What a horrible movie!这电影太恐怖了！
  • What a beautiful child.多么漂亮的孩子！
3 
— used to refer to an amount that someone has, uses, etc.全部的；所有的
  • She gave what money she had [=she gave all the money she had] to the homeless man.她将她所有的钱都给了这个无家可归的男人。
  • Give what excuses you will—it makes no difference. [=no excuses that you may give will make any difference]无论你有什么借口都无济于事。
  • He soon gambled away what (little) money he had left.他很快就赌光了仅剩的（那点）钱。
  • We spent what (little) time remained chatting.剩下的（那点）时间里我们用来聊天了。

so what

informal
— used to say that something said or done is not important那又怎样（用于表示所说或所做的事情不重要）
  • She has a glass of wine now and then—so what?她偶尔喝杯红酒，那又怎样？

what with

— used to introduce the part of a sentence that indicates the cause of something由于，因为（用于引导句子成分，表原因）
  • What with the freezing temperatures, they nearly died.因为严寒，他们差点死了。
  • What with school and sports, she's always busy.因为要学习和运动，她总是很忙。
  • Things have been difficult for him lately, what with his wife's illness and all.因为妻子生病等原因，他最近的处境一直很困难。
