musty

/ˈmʌstɪ/
adjective

mustier, mustiest

1
having a stale, mouldy, or damp smell
有霉味的; 有潮气的：

a dark musty library filled with old books.

昏暗又散发着霉味的藏书室, 里面堆满了旧书。

1.1
having a stale taste
霉的, 发霉的; 有霉味的：

the beer tasted sour, thin, and musty.

啤酒味道又酸又淡, 还有股霉味。

1.2
figurative lacking originality or interest
〈喻〉陈腐的, 缺乏独创性的; 乏味的：

when I read it again, the play seemed musty.

我再次阅读剧本时, 觉得它没有一点新意。

派生词

mustily

adverb

mustiness

noun
词源
early 16th cent.: perhaps an alteration of moisty 'moist', influenced by MUST2.
