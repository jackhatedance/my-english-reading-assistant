twelve

/twelv/
cardinal number
1
equivalent to the product of three and four; two more than ten;
12十二; 十二个：

he walked twelve miles

他走了12英里

there are just twelve of us in all

我们总共只有12人

a twelve-string guitar

12弦吉他。

(Roman numeral: xii or XII. 罗马数字：xii或XII。)

1.1
a group or unit of twelve people or things
十二个人（或物）一组。
1.2
twelve years old
十二岁：

a small blonde girl of about twelve.

一个约12岁的金发小女孩。

1.3
twelve o'clock
十二点钟：

it's half past twelve.

十二点半。

1.4
a size of garment or other merchandise denoted by twelve
（衣服等商品的）十二号。
1.5

the Twelve

the twelve Apostles
十二门徒。
1.6

12

Brit. (of a film) classified as suitable for people of 12 years and over
〈英〉适于十二岁及以上人观看的电影。
词源
Old English twelf（e）, from the base of TWO + a second element （probably expressing the sense 'left over'）; of Germanic origin and related to Dutch twaalf and German zwölf. Compare with ELEVEN.
