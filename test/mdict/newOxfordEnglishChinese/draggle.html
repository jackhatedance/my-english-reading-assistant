draggle

/ˈdrægl/
verb
with obj.
1
make (something) dirty or wet, typically by trailing it through mud or water
拖脏, 拖湿：

as adj.draggled she wore a draggled skirt.

她穿着一条拖脏了的长裙。

1.1
no obj. hang untidily
凌乱地悬挂着：

red hairs draggled dispiritedly from her chignon.

几根红发无精打采地从她的发髻松垂下来。

1.2
no obj. archaic trail behind others; lag behind
〈古〉落在后面, 掉队; 缓慢地跟在后面：

they draggled at the heels of his troop.

他们慢吞吞地跟在他部队的后面。

词源
early 16th cent.: diminutive and frequentative of DRAG.
